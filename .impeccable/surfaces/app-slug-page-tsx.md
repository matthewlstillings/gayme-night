# Rulebook, quick guide and hub

Scope: the hub (/), every rulebook (/[slug]) and every quick guide (/[slug]/guide). Mode: Read. Audience: Matthew's game night group, at the table, phone in hand, dim room. Job: find the exact rule fast; new players learn from the quick guide.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The rulebook is a pocket field guide read by flashlight: calm ink-navy pages, plates beside the text, thumb-index tabs on the edge. The quick guide is the player aid card that sits beside the board. Refuses the generic dark docs site with a neon accent.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-navy ground (#0f1420), raised plate panels, hairline rules, Literata reading serif, Atkinson Hyperlegible for UI and the quick guide body, Barlow Condensed for tabs, bands and numerals. Each game's own colour lives in the thumb tabs, plate labels and running heads on rulebooks, and fills the full numbered section bands on quick guides.

STORY: The reader opens a game, searches or taps a page tab, reads the rule in a calm column with its diagram labelled as a plate beside it, and gets back to the game. A new player opens the quick guide and reads it band by band.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rulebook: header (back, game name in serif with a small-caps Rulebook label in game colour, quick guide, search, contents), a 2px game-colour rule under it; contents left on desktop, reading column centre at about 68ch with a running head per page (small caps PAGE 4 and a hairline), and the thumb-index rail of page tabs fixed on the right edge, current page filled. Quick guide: same header, then section 1 as a full-width game-colour band with a big condensed numeral and the heading in dark ink. Hub: one grid of field-guide entries, each with an index tab in its game colour.
