How to win

Finish your secret Objective and get your Character out alive. Several players can win.

1. Complete your ObjectiveYou start with 2 secret Objectives (1 Personal, 1 Corporate). At the first Intruder Encounter, everyone keeps 1 and discards the other.
2. SurviveLock yourself in the Isolation Room (and the Facility isn't destroyed), launch out in a CSS Pod, or reach the Bunker.

When the game ends, every survivor goes through the Victory Check (p. 12):

  1. Contingency check: the secret Active Contingency is revealed and may kill some survivors.
  2. Contamination check: Scan every Contamination card in your deck, discard pile and hand. If any says INFECTED (or you have a Larva), shuffle all your cards and draw 4. Any Contamination card drawn means death.
  3. Objective check: reveal your Objective and see if you met it.
Semi-co-opWorking together helps, but other players' wins don't count for you. You can't attack other Characters directly, but you can harm them as a side effect (fire, Autodestruction, a grenade).

Player Phase

Everyone draws to 5, the First Player token moves left, then you take turns of 2 Actions until everyone has passed.

Phase I In this order

  1. 1Draw to 5Everyone draws Action cards until they hold 5 (6 if you start the round in the Isolation Room). If your deck is empty, shuffle your discard pile into a new deck.
  2. 2Pass the First Player token leftSkip this in the first round. Anything done "in order" starts with the First Player and goes clockwise.
  3. 3Take turns of 2 ActionsStarting with the First Player and going clockwise, each player takes a turn of 2 Actions. Keep going around until everyone has passed, then go to the Event Phase.

Paying for Actions

  • Cost: the number in an Action's symbol is how many Action cards you discard from hand to pay for it.
  • Action cards: to use one, discard that card plus its Cost. A card with [OR] lets you pick one option.
  • Combat symbols: only in Combat, only out of Combat. No symbol means either. You're in Combat when an Intruder is in your Room (Eggs don't count).
  • Contamination cards sit in your hand but can't pay for anything. You can only discard them when you pass.
  • Interrupted? You still pay the Cost.

Passing and ending your turn

  • Only 1 Action? Then your second Action is a Pass. Passing counts as an Action.
  • When you pass: you may discard any cards from hand, flip your Help card to its Pass side, and you take no more Actions this round.
  • Fire: end your turn in a Room with Fire and you take 1 Light Wound. Only once per round.

Moving and Noise

Entering an empty Room means a Noise roll. Noise in a Corridor that already has Noise means an Intruder appears.

When you enter a Room

  1. Unexplored? Flip the Room tile and reveal its Exploration token . Turn the tile so the token's number points at the Item Counter: that's how many Searches the Room has. Then resolve its effect (below).
  2. Nobody there? Roll the Noise die. If another Character or an Intruder is already in the Room, skip the roll.

Exploration token effects

TokenEffect

SlimePut a Slime marker on your Character board.

Things in your way

  • Doors : a standing Door is Closed and blocks movement. An Intruder that tries to go through destroys it instead (it lies down and can't close again). No token means Open.
  • Technical Corridors: Characters can't use them (only Vents, the Cave Entrance and Cave Plans can). Noise on the Technical Corridors space counts at every Entrance on the board.
  • Emergency Staircases between Sections are always dark and have no Action.
  • Elevator (cost 1, needs Power, out of Combat): call it to your Section, or ride it to another Section's Elevator Room with anyone who agrees. No Noise roll after.
  • Malfunction turns off a Room's Action and its Computer. Searching still works.

Encounters and Combat

When an Intruder appears, count the cards in your hand. Fewer than the token's number means a Surprise Attack.

Encounter

  1. Discard all Noise markers from the Corridors connected to the Room.
  2. Draw an Intruder token from the bag and place that Intruder in the Room.
  3. Compare your hand (Contamination cards count) to the token : the lower number in a Section with Power, the higher one in Darkness. If your hand is smaller, the Intruder makes a Surprise Attack.
  • Blank token : put Noise in every connected Corridor instead. If it was the last token in the bag, add an Adult token. Return the Blank.
  • Walking into an Intruder's Room isn't an Encounter. You're simply in Combat.
  • First Encounter of the game: everyone discards 1 Objective. See Notable events.

Shoot

Spend 1 Ammo from a Weapon in your Hand and roll the Combat die. In a Section with Power you must roll the Advantage die instead.

Melee Attack: take a Contamination card first, then roll the normal Combat die (never the Advantage die).

Did it die?

  • Larva or Egg: 1 Injury kills it.
  • Creeper or Adult: draw 1 Intruder Attack card and read only its blood number . Injuries equal to or above it kill. Put a Carcass token where it died.
  • Breeder or Queen: same, but add up the blood numbers on 2 cards.
  • Retreat arrow on any drawn card: it flees instead. Draw an Event card and move it through that Corridor number. It keeps its Injuries.

Intruder Attacks

  • When: a Surprise Attack, the Event Phase attack step, or when you Escape.
  • Who: the Character whose player holds the fewest Action cards. Ties go to the First Player, then turn order. A Surprise Attack hits whoever caused it; an Escape hits whoever is escaping.
  • How: draw an Intruder Attack card. If it shows the attacker's symbol, resolve it (plus its Darkness part in a Section without Power). If not, it misses.
  • Larva: draws no card. It jumps onto your Character board (if you don't have one) and you take a Contamination card.

Escape

Use Movement to leave a Room with an Intruder. Each Intruder in the Room attacks you first. If you survive, you move (and Explore or roll Noise as normal).

Wounds

  • Light Wounds move a marker down your track. The third one turns into a Serious Wound.
  • Serious Wounds are cards with a lasting effect. Dressing one flips it so its effect stops, but it still counts. Healing removes a Light Wound or a Dressed card.
  • Death: any Wound after your third Serious Wound kills you. See Notable events.

Contamination

  • New Contamination cards go on top of your discard pile and clog your hand later. Some are secretly INFECTED.
  • Scanning: lay the red Scanner over the card. If one line reads INFECTED, put a Larva on your board and keep the card. Already have a Larva? You die and a Creeper appears. Some words look like INFECTED, so read closely.
  • Getting clean: Rest (Action card), the Decon Room, Surgery and the Antiseptic Item.

Items, Search and Crafting

Search a Room for Items of its colour, keep weapons in your Hands, and combine 2 Items into a Crafted Item.

Searching

  • Colours: red is military, yellow technical, green medical. A Room's colour is the one behind its name. In a white Room, pick any of the three decks.
  • Each Search lowers the Room's Item Counter by 1. At 0 the Room is empty. Special Rooms and the Nest can't be searched.
  • Draw and keep: the rulebook's example (p. 26) draws 2 and keeps 1. Your Search card has the exact rule. Put an unused Item face down at the bottom of its deck.

Carrying

  • Heavy : Items with the Hand symbol and all Objects (Corpse, Carcass, Egg) go in your 2 Hand slots, face up. Weapons come loaded: start Ammo / max Ammo.
  • Everything else goes face down in your Inventory, with no limit. Reveal it when you use it.
  • One Use Only (yellow text): discard the Item after using it.
  • Drop anything for free on your turn. Objects stay in the Room; Items go back to the bottom of their deck.
  • Quest Items start sideways. Meet the condition on the card to flip it and use it.

Crafting recipes

Craft Item (cost 1, out of Combat): discard 2 Items whose blue part symbols match the grey symbols on a Crafted Item card, first symbol then second. Take that card from the blue deck. You can look through the blue deck any time.

Event Phase

Once everyone has passed, the Facility and the Intruders act. Do these 8 steps in order.

Phase II In this order

  1. 4Launch CSSIf a CSS token is under the Time token, reveal it: it says which Pods launch (below).
  2. 5Move the Time token 1 rightThen check Autodestruction and the Alert Procedure, and whether you crossed a Power Threshold.
  3. 6Remove NoiseIn Sections with Power only: remove Noise from Corridors not connected to a Room with a Character. Never from the Technical Corridors space.
  4. 7Intruders attackEvery Intruder in a Room with a Character attacks. See Intruder Attacks.
  5. 8Fire damageEach Intruder in a Room with Fire takes 1 Injury. Fire in the Nest destroys 1 uncarried Egg.
  6. 9Resolve an Event cardMove Intruders first, then apply the effect (below).
  7. 10Develop the Intruder bagDraw 1 Intruder token and resolve it (below).
  8. 11New roundOnce step 10 is fully done, including any Encounters, start the next Player Phase.

Step 5: Time and Power

  • Autodestruction and Alert Procedure: see Notable events.
  • Power Thresholds are marked on the Time track. While the Time token is red (no backup power), every Threshold it crosses cuts Power. Once it's flipped blue , only Thresholds with two Time token symbols do.
  • Cutting Power: flip the active Power token with the highest number to red . Blue means that Section has Power.

Step 9: Event card

  1. Move Intruders: every Intruder whose symbol is on the card and that isn't in a Room with a Character moves through the Corridor with the card's number. With two numbers, move through the first, then the second (stopping if it reaches a Character).
  2. Technical Corridor: the Intruder leaves the board and its token goes back in the bag.
  3. Closed Door: the Intruder stays put and destroys the Door.
  4. Effect: apply the card's effect. Its Darkness part only applies in Sections without Power.

Notable events

These can happen in any round. Each one changes the game for everyone.

First Encounter

The first time an Intruder miniature appears (Eggs don't count), everyone secretly discards 1 of their 2 Objectives. Then finish the Encounter.

The lights go out

  • Darkness: a Section without Power. Event and Attack cards get extra effects, and Encounters use the higher number on the Intruder token. A Flashlight or Glowstick can help.
  • Restoring Power: the Backup Power Supply resets and rearranges the Power tokens and flips the Time token to blue, which slows the blackout. The Power Generator powers your Section.

Knowledge and Weaknesses

  • Knowledge : +3 for analysing an Object in the Laboratory (or with the Analyze Toolset), +2 from the Archive (which flips your token ), +1 for discovering the Nest or from some Computer Actions.
  • Weaknesses: analysing a Corpse, Carcass or Egg in the Laboratory reveals the next Weakness card. Each type of Object can reveal only one. They help Characters with Knowledge 3, 5 or 8.

A Character dies

  • Their Corpse stays in the Room and their Heavy Objects drop there. Their other Items leave the game and their Contingency tokens go to the Neutral Pool.
  • The first death opens the Main Gate, so the Bunker becomes reachable.
  • Infected with a Larva already on your board means death, and a Creeper appears where you died.

Ways out open up

  • CSS Pods: enter one from a Cargo Sending System Room while the Time token is on a CSS slot. You discard your cards and pass, and the Pod tries to launch in the Event Phase.
  • Isolation Room: opens when the Time token reaches the white slot 8, or earlier through the Alert Procedure. Locking yourself in needs a Noise roll with no Intruder appearing.
  • Bunker: needs the Main Gate open, you in the Exit Room with no Intruder, and the Rover there or an Enviro-Suit.

Alert Procedure

Started in the Alert Room. Put the Alert token on the Time track slot equal to the current slot divided by 2 (rounded down). The Isolation Room opens at once. The game ends as soon as the Time token leaves the Alert token's slot.

Autodestruction

  • Started in the Cooling System: the token goes 3 slots ahead of the Time token, yellow side up.
  • Yellow: anyone can stop it in the Power Generator Room.
  • When the Time token reaches it, it jumps 3 slots ahead and flips red. The Main Gate opens and it can't be stopped.
  • Red reached: the Facility explodes. Everyone inside dies, including in the Isolation Room.

Contingency

One token is secretly Active and judges every survivor at the end. You can look at your own; you can trade, give and lie about what they say. Peek at others with the Archive, the Alert Room (Neutral Pool) or a Computer Action (all of one player's tokens in your Section). The Active one can never be checked.

The game ends

  • Time runs out (the final red slot): everyone not in the Isolation Room dies. The Intruders survive.
  • The Facility explodes (red Autodestruction, a 13th Fire or an 11th Malfunction marker): everyone dies, including in the Isolation Room, and so do the Intruders and the Queen.
  • Nobody is left inside: the last Character dies, isolates, escapes or launches. Jump the Time token to the Autodestruction or Alert token if one is out, otherwise to the end.

Once you escape, isolate or die, you only watch. If anyone survived, go to the Victory Check.

Tips for new players

From the rulebook

  • Pick your Character with your Objectives in mind. You get Objectives before the draft for this reason.
  • Keep cards in hand when you're heading into unknown or dark areas. A full hand is your shield against Surprise Attacks.
  • Moving in a group skips Noise rolls, but events that make everyone roll can hit a group hard.
  • Scan your Contamination before the end. An unscanned infection can lose you the game at the last moment.
  • Trade information about Contingencies. It can be traded, given and lied about. Use Help cards to share it secretly.
  • Lab Rat and Survivor start with 3 Knowledge, so they can use the first Weakness from the start.

From the FAQ Publisher FAQ

  • Items work on yourself only. To use a Medkit, Clothes or similar on someone else, trade it to them first.
  • Several Characters can share the Isolation Room. It has multiple chambers.
  • The Archive works once per Character, because it flips your Knowledge token to active.
  • Danger still happens on Careful Movement when you reveal a Danger Exploration token.

From the "official game FAQ" for Nemesis: Lockdown (March 2022), hosted by a German retailer: Lockdown FAQ PDF.

From experienced players Unofficial

  • Stick together early and use Careful Movement when you can afford it, to keep Noise and alien numbers down.
  • Search early for weapons and crafting parts so a late Encounter doesn't catch you empty-handed.
  • Keep Fire and Malfunction counts low. Running out of markers ends the game for everyone.
  • Run before you shoot if you're short on cards. Shooting first can leave you no cards to Escape.
  • Bring a Corpse or Carcass to the Laboratory when you pass one, to unlock Weaknesses.
