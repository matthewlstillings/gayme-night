A cheat sheet for new players, summarised from the official Nemesis: Lockdown rulebook. Each section names the rulebook page to check when a question comes up.

Tips at the end are marked by where they come from: the rulebook itself, the publisher's FAQ, or experienced players.

Start hereRulebook p. 10–12

How to win

Finish your secret Objective and get your Character out alive. Several players can win.

1. Complete your ObjectiveYou start with 2 secret Objectives (1 Personal, 1 Corporate). At the first Intruder Encounter, everyone keeps 1 and discards the other.
2. SurviveLock yourself in the Isolation Room (and the Facility isn't destroyed), launch out in a CSS Pod, or reach the Bunker.

Then you still have to pass the Victory Check:

  1. Contingency check: the secret Active Contingency is revealed and may kill some survivors.
  2. Contamination check: Scan your Contamination cards. If any says INFECTED (or you have a Larva), shuffle your deck and draw 4. Any Contamination card drawn means death.
  3. Objective check: reveal your Objective and see if you met it.
Semi-co-opWorking together helps, but other players' wins don't count for you. You can't attack other Characters directly, but you can harm them as a side effect (fire, Autodestruction, a grenade).
The loopRulebook p. 13

How a round works

Every round is a Player Phase (everyone acts) then an Event Phase (the Facility and the aliens act).

Phase I Player Phase

  1. 1Draw to 5Everyone draws Action cards until they hold 5. If your deck is empty, shuffle your discard pile into a new deck.
  2. 2Pass the First Player token leftSkip this in the first round.
  3. 3Take turns of 2 ActionsStarting with the First Player and going clockwise, each player takes a turn of 2 Actions. Keep going around until everyone has passed.

Phase II Event Phase

  1. 4Launch CSSIf a CSS token sits under the Time token, reveal it: it says which CSS Pods launch.
  2. 5Advance the Time trackMove the Time token 1 slot. Check Autodestruction and the Alert Procedure, and whether a Power Threshold drops the Power.
  3. 6Remove NoiseIn Sections with Power only: remove Noise markers from Corridors not connected to a Room with a Character. Never from the Technical Corridors space.
  4. 7Intruders attackEvery Intruder in a Room with a Character attacks.
  5. 8Fire damages IntrudersEach Intruder in a Room with Fire takes 1 Injury.
  6. 9Resolve an Event cardFirst move the Intruders it shows (not ones in Combat) through the Corridor number on the card, then apply its effect. Darkness effects apply only in Sections without Power.
  7. 10Intruder bag developmentDraw 1 token. Larva becomes an Adult, Creeper becomes a Breeder, Adult or Breeder makes everyone not in Combat roll for Noise, Queen can appear in the Nest (or adds an Egg), Blank adds an Adult.
  8. 11New roundOnce everything from step 10 is resolved, start the next round.
When it's youRulebook p. 13–14

How your turn works

Do 2 Actions, paying for them by discarding Action cards. When you can't or don't want to act, pass.

  • Paying: the number in an Action's symbol is how many Action cards you discard from hand to pay for it.
  • Action cards: to use one, discard that card plus its Cost. A card with [OR] lets you pick one option.
  • Contamination cards sit in your hand but can't pay for anything. You can only discard them when you pass.
  • Only 1 Action? Then your second Action is a Pass.
  • Passing: you may discard any cards from hand, flip your Help card to its Pass side, and you take no more Actions this round. Passing counts as an Action.
  • Fire: if you end your turn in a Room with Fire, you take 1 Light Wound (only once per round).
Watch outEvery card you spend shrinks your hand, and a small hand is what triggers Surprise Attacks. See Encounters.

Where danger startsRulebook p. 16–19

Moving, exploring and Noise

Entering an empty Room means a Noise roll. Noise in a Corridor that already has Noise means an alien appears.

When you enter a Room

  1. Unexplored? Flip the Room tile, then reveal its Exploration token. It sets how many Items the Room holds and has an effect: Silence, Danger, Slime, Fire, Malfunction or Door.
  2. Empty Room? Roll the Noise die. If another Character or an Intruder is already there, skip the roll.

Noise roll results

  • 1–4: put a Noise marker in that numbered Corridor. If it already has one, an Encounter happens instead.
  • Danger: Intruders in neighbouring Rooms (not in Combat) move into your Room. If there are none, put Noise in every connected Corridor that doesn't have one.
  • Silence: nothing happens, unless you have Slime on you, which turns Silence into Danger.

Other things on the board

  • Technical Corridors: Characters can't use them (with a few card exceptions). Noise there counts at every Technical Corridor Entrance on the board.
  • Closed Doors block movement. An Intruder that tries to go through destroys the Door instead of moving.
  • Fire and Malfunction pools: if you need to place a 13th Fire marker or an 11th Malfunction marker, the Facility explodes and everyone dies.
  • Malfunction in a Room turns off its Room Action (and its Computer). Searching still works.
When aliens show upRulebook p. 20–22

Encounters and Combat

When an alien appears, count the cards in your hand. Fewer than the token's number means a Surprise Attack.

Encounter

  1. Discard all Noise markers from the Corridors connected to the Room.
  2. Draw an Intruder token from the bag and place that Intruder in the Room.
  3. Compare your hand size (Contamination cards count) to the token's number: the lower number normally, the higher one in Darkness. If your hand is smaller, the Intruder makes a Surprise Attack.

A Blank token instead puts Noise in every connected Corridor. Walking into a Room that has an Intruder is not an Encounter; you're just in Combat.

In Combat (same Room as an Intruder)

  • Shoot or Melee Attack it, or Escape by moving out. Escaping triggers an Intruder Attack first, one for each Intruder in the Room.
  • Did it die? Larvae and Eggs die from 1 Injury. For a Creeper or Adult, draw 1 Intruder Attack card and read only its blood number: Injuries equal to or above it kill. For a Breeder or Queen, add up 2 cards. A Retreat arrow on the card means it flees instead.
  • Who gets attacked? The Character whose player holds the fewest Action cards. Ties go to the First Player, then turn order.
  • Larva attack: it doesn't draw a card. It jumps onto your Character board and you take a Contamination card.
Staying aliveRulebook p. 22–23

Wounds and Contamination

The third Light Wound becomes a Serious Wound. Any Wound after your third Serious Wound kills you.

  • Light Wounds move a marker down your track. The third one turns into a Serious Wound.
  • Serious Wounds are cards with a lasting penalty. Dressing one flips it so its effect stops, but it still counts toward the 3.
  • On death: your Corpse token stays in the Room and your Heavy Objects drop there. The Main Gate opens the first time anyone dies.
  • Contamination cards go on top of your discard pile and clog your hand later. Some are secretly INFECTED.
  • Scanning: the red Scanner shows the word INFECTED. If infected, put a Larva on your board. If you're infected and already have a Larva, you die and a Creeper appears.
  • Getting clean: Rest, the Decon Room, the Surgery Room and the Antiseptic Item all help. Look closely when Scanning; some words look like INFECTED.
New in LockdownRulebook p. 24

Power and Darkness

The Facility loses Power as time passes. Dark Sections are much more dangerous.

  • Sections with Power (blue Power token): you must Shoot with the Advantage die, Computer Actions work, you're not in Darkness, and Noise gets cleared in the Event Phase.
  • Darkness (no Power): extra effects on Event and Attack cards, and the higher number on Intruder tokens decides Surprise Attacks.
  • Emergency Staircases between Sections are always dark. A Glowstick or Flashlight helps.
  • Power Thresholds on the Time track switch off Sections as the Time token passes. The Backup Power Supply Room (S-03) slows that down.
TriggersRulebook p. 10–11

Big moments

  • First Encounter: the first time an Intruder miniature appears, everyone secretly discards 1 Objective.
  • First death: the Main Gate opens, so the Bunker becomes reachable.
  • Isolation Room: usable once the Time token reaches the white slot 8 (earlier if someone activates the Alert Room).
  • Autodestruction: started in the Cooling System. While its token is yellow, it can be stopped in the Power Generator Room. Once it flips red, it can't be stopped.
  • Contingency: one token is secretly Active and judges survivors at the end. You see your own token; peek at others with the Archive, Alert Room or Computer to work out which one is Active.
How it stopsRulebook p. 12

When the game ends

  • Time runs out (the final red slot): the Facility is purged. Everyone not in the Isolation Room dies, but the Intruders survive.
  • The Facility explodes (red Autodestruction, a 13th Fire or an 11th Malfunction marker): everyone dies, including in the Isolation Room, and so do the Intruders and Queen.
  • Nobody is left inside: the last Character dies, isolates, escapes or launches.

Once you escape, isolate or die, you only watch until the end.

Tips for new players

From the rulebook

  • Pick your Character with your Objectives in mind. You get Objectives before the draft for this reason.
  • Keep cards in hand when you're heading into unknown or dark areas. A full hand is your shield against Surprise Attacks.
  • Moving in a group skips Noise rolls, but events that make everyone roll can hit a group hard.
  • Scan your Contamination before the end. An unscanned infection can lose you the game at the last moment.
  • Trade information about Contingencies. It can be traded, given and lied about. Use Help cards to share it secretly.
  • Lab Rat and Survivor start with 3 Knowledge, so they can use the first Weakness from the start.

From the FAQ Publisher FAQ

  • Items work on yourself only. To use a Medkit, Clothes or similar on someone else, trade it to them first.
  • Several Characters can share the Isolation Room. It has multiple chambers.
  • The Archive works once per Character, because it flips your Knowledge token to active.
  • Danger still happens on Careful Movement when you reveal a Danger Exploration token.

From the "official game FAQ" for Nemesis: Lockdown (March 2022), hosted by a German retailer: Lockdown FAQ PDF.

From experienced players Unofficial

  • Stick together early and use Careful Movement when you can afford it, to keep Noise and alien numbers down.
  • Search early for weapons and crafting parts so a late Encounter doesn't catch you empty-handed.
  • Keep Fire and Malfunction counts low. Running out of markers ends the game for everyone.
  • Run before you shoot if you're short on cards. Shooting first can leave you no cards to Escape.
  • Bring a Corpse or Carcass to the Laboratory when you pass one, to unlock Weaknesses.
