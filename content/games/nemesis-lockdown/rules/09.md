### Discard Piles

Leave an empty space for a discard pile next to each of the following decks: Items, Event, Attack and Serious Wounds.

*Note: There's a special spot for the Action discard pile, marked on each of the Character boards. Contamination cards go the the player's Action discard pile until they are removed from the game.*

All the above cards are discarded face up.

All **markers** are discarded to the appropriate pile.
