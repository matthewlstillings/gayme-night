## Dragon Abilities

[[page]]

All dragons have an ability or benefit printed on them. These come in 4 types:

- **When played:** When you play a dragon with a "when played" benefit, you may gain the indicated benefit immediately. Unless you have a special ability, you will only activate this ability once. These benefits are not mandatory, though it is usually in your best interest to gain them. If you play a dragon that gives opponents a benefit, the other players must be allowed to collect their benefit, even if you decline yours.
- **If activated (adventurer icon):** Whenever your adventurer walks onto a dragon card with an "if activated" ability, you may activate that ability. If you have an "if activated" ability that gives opponents a benefit, the other players must be allowed to collect that benefit, even if you decline to do so.
- **Once per round (stopwatch icon):** "Once per round" abilities provide a benefit at the end of every round. See additional details in the Round End/Upkeep section. If you have a "once per round" ability that gives opponents a benefit, the other players must be allowed to collect that benefit, even if you decline to do so.
- **End game (flag icon):** "End game" abilities provide Victory Points (VP) at the end of the game for meeting certain conditions.

> **Important:** "If activated" abilities do not activate the turn that you play the dragon.

### Example Dragon Card

The example is the Bellicose Firevern:

- **Cost (upper left):** To play this dragon, pay 1 meat and 1 gold. (If you used the entice action to play this dragon, you also must pay 1 coin.)
- **Caves (left side):** Caves where this dragon can live are indicated on the left side of the dragon card. This dragon can live in the Crimson Cavern or the Golden Grotto.
- **Ability (bottom):** Each dragon provides an ability or benefit. This dragon provides a "once per round" ability.
- **VP (upper right):** Earn 5 VP for playing this dragon.
- **Size:** Dragons come in 4 sizes: Large, Medium, Small, and Hatchling.
- **Nests:** Egg storage capacity. Dragons can store 1 egg on each nest icon they have (e.g., this dragon has 1 nest icon).
- **Trait:** Dragons have 1 of 4 traits: shy, aggressive, playful, or helpful.

> **Important:** Whenever a dragon looks for a specific category (e.g., in this cave, a trait, a size, etc.), the dragon includes itself if applicable. For example, the Smirking Ridgeback (card 130) looks for playful dragons to tuck a card under; because the Smirking Ridgeback is a playful dragon, a card is tucked under it too.
