The loopRulebook p. 4, 14

How a round works

Take turns clockwise, 1 action each, paying with coins. Keep going until you pass. The round ends when everyone has passed.

  • Passing: usually once you're out of coins or have nothing worth playing. You can still gain benefits on others' turns.
  • Carry over: leftover coins, resources, cards and nested eggs go into the next round.

Round end In this order

  1. 1Clear your matDiscard the coins and eggs you paid as costs on your mat.
  2. 2Once-per-round dragons fireIn turn order from the round's start player. Your own in any order.
  3. 3Score the round's objectivePlace a marker under it by your rank. Ties share the place. A "zero" goes on "all others".

New round Before the first turn

  2. 5Refresh the displayDiscard the 3 dragons and 3 cave cards and deal new ones.
  3. 6IncomeEveryone gains 6 coins and 1 egg. No free nest? You lose the egg.
  4. 7Pass the start player marker1 player clockwise.

Pick 1 action

Move your adventurer to the action, pay its cost onto your mat, and do it.

ActionCostWhat it does
ExcavateCave card1 coinPlay a cave card into the leftmost empty space of a cave. Column 3 also costs 1 egg, column 4 costs 2 eggs.
EnticeDragon1 coinPay the dragon's printed cost and play it into an excavated space in one of its caves, left to right.
ExploreOne cave1 coinWalk your adventurer left to right through one cave, using every "if activated" ability you pass.
  • Excavate bonuses: take the cave card's "when played" benefit and the covered space's benefit, in either order.
  • Exploring again in the same round costs more: 1 coin and 1 egg the 2nd time, 1 coin and 2 eggs the 3rd (the max).
  • Explore stops at a stop sign (a space without a dragon) or the end of the cave. Your adventurer goes back to Base Camp.
Turn endDiscard down to 9 coins, 9 cards and 9 resource tokens.
What dragons doRulebook p. 7

Dragon abilities

Four kinds. All optional, and "all players" ones let everyone join in even if you skip.

  • When played: once, right after you play the dragon.
  • If activated (adventurer): when you explore onto the dragon. Not on the turn you play it.
  • Once per round (stopwatch): at every round end.
  • End game (flag): points at final scoring.
Counts itselfA dragon looking for a category ("in this cave", a trait, a size) includes itself if it matches.
Words to knowRulebook p. 4, 8, 11

Eggs, cache and tuck

  • Resources are gold, crystal, meat and milk. Coins and eggs are not resources.
  • 2-for-1: at any time, trade any 2 resources for 1 of another type.
  • Lay an egg: on an empty nest icon on a dragon or your mat. No empty nest, no egg.
  • Cache: put a resource on a dragon. It stays there and is worth 1 point.
  • Tuck: slide a dragon card face down under a dragon. Worth 1 point, but it doesn't count as a dragon.
  • Hatchlings: each cache or tuck gives a benefit. The 3rd time also gives a one-time grow-up bonus.
The side trackRulebook p. 12–13

The Dragon Guild

Each guild advancement moves your token 1 space clockwise. Gain the benefit where it lands.

  • Several steps at once: gain every benefit you pass, plus the one you land on.
  • Brown spaces: reaching one means placing a player marker on the Dragon Guild tile and taking that square's benefit.
  • Max 4 markers on the tile. After that you can still advance around the track.
  • Same turn? Players who reach a brown space together pick in clockwise order from the active player.
After round 4Rulebook p. 15

Final scoring

  1. Your markers on the Dragon Guild with end game benefits.
  2. Printed points on visible dragons on your mat.
  3. End game abilities on visible dragons.
  4. 1 point per egg, cached resource and tucked dragon.
  5. Public objectives. Ties are friendly: everyone at a level takes its full points.
  6. 1 point per leftover coin, and 1 point per 4 other leftover items (round down).
Coming from WingspanRulebook p. 6

If you already know Wingspan

  • Actions cost coins (and often eggs), not action cubes.
  • Explore left to right, not right to left.
  • Excavate before you entice. You start with 3 excavated spaces.
  • Your mat has 2 nests before you play any dragon.
  • Start with 4 cards chosen from 3 dragons and 3 caves, plus any 3 resources.
  • Objectives are friendly: tied players both get full points.
Play betterRulebook p. 2, 4–5

Tips

  • Spend most or all of your coins before you pass. You get 6 more each round.
  • Pick starting resources after looking at your cards. Match the dragons you kept.
  • Excavate the 4th column early. Building out each cave can beat waiting for the coin trade.
