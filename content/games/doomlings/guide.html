How to win

Have the most points when the 3rd catastrophe flips and the world ends.

Face valueThe number on every trait in your trait pile.
Bonus pointsDrop of Life bonuses on your traits, plus the last catastrophe's gold effect.
Tie?Casual game: share the win. Serious game: each tied player draws a trait, and the highest face value wins.

The loopRulebook p. 3

How a round works

Flip an age, then everyone takes 1 turn clockwise. Repeat until the 3rd catastrophe.

Your turn 3 steps

  1. APlay 1 traitFrom your hand into your trait pile, in front of you.
  2. BUse its effectIf it has one and you can play it, do it now.
  3. CStabilizeDraw or discard until your hand matches the top number on your Gene Pool.

Next age After everyone's turn

  1. 1Flip the next agePlay its effect. A rule lasts this round only. Gene Pool changes are permanent.
  2. 2Catastrophe 1 or 2?First player shifts 1 left. Play only the white text, and start a new era pile after the turns.
Hand sizeRulebook p. 5

Gene Pool and stabilizing

The number at the top of your Gene Pool is your hand size. You match it only at the end of your turn.

  • +1 or -1 Gene Pool: rotate or flip the card. Don't stabilize right away.
  • Lose the trait, lose the change: adjust your Gene Pool back if that trait leaves your pile.
  • Limits: never above 8 or below 1. Ignore effects that would break that.

Gold starRulebook p. 6

Dominant traits

Max 2 per game. Once played, nothing can move or remove them.

One exceptionHolding Heroic when the age Birth of a Hero flips? You may play it as a 3rd dominant.
Moving cardsRulebook p. 6

Steals, swaps and restrictions

  • From a hand goes to your hand. From a trait pile goes to your trait pile. Same for moved or given cards.
  • Restricted? If an age blocks part of an effect, you can't play a trait that requires it. Otherwise just skip that part.
3rd catastropheRulebook p. 4

World's End and scoring

No more turns. Do these in order, then count.

  1. 1World's End traitsIn turn order, play effects with the flag icon.
  2. 2Gold textEveryone follows the catastrophe's gold text. Ignore its white text.
  3. 3ScoreGold effect points, plus face values, plus Drop of Life bonuses.
Common questionsRulebook p. 8

When you're stuck

  • You must play a trait if you can, even one you don't want.
  • Empty hand: draw 5 and skip your turn.
  • Can't play anything: skip, or discard your hand and draw 3. Don't stabilize either way.
  • Trait deck empty: shuffle in the discard pile. If that's gone too, end turns without stabilizing and jump to the 3rd catastrophe after the round.
  • Disputes: ages beat traits, traits beat the rulebook. Order questions follow turn order.
Mix it upRulebook p. 9

Variants

  • Stunted Growth (shorter): no turns on catastrophes.
  • Prosperous World (longer, 2–4): 3 piles of 4 ages, or 4 piles of 3 ages, each with 1 catastrophe.
  • Strategic Start: draft your opening 5 cards.
  • Intelligent Design (2–4): draft your own 28-card trait deck.
  • Genetic Mishap: solo mode with a Lab.
