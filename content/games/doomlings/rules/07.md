## Trait Icons

[[page]]

Icons identify different types of trait effects.

- **Actions** (the A icon) are effects that occur only when played *directly from your hand*. If you can play the action, you must. If you can't, ignore it. By default, do **not** play actions on traits you steal or swap, *unless* you're told to do so.
- **Requirements** (the exclamation icon) that say "To play, you must…" must be fulfilled in order to play certain traits. Once played, they can be swapped, stolen, or given without restriction.
- **Play When** cards (the winged icon) can be played out of turn. Pay close attention to the effect's text! (They can also be played normally during your turn, but the effect may not apply.)
- **Persistent** effects (the circling arrows icon) are triggered by a future event and may reoccur. Pay close attention to the text!
- **World's End** effects (the flag icon) are only played during the 3rd catastrophe at the end of the game.
- **Drop of Life** bonuses (the droplet icon) are counted during scoring at the end of the game.

## Trait Colors

There are **4 colors** that you can find on any trait in the game: **blue, green, purple, and red**. Grey cards are "colorless" and *cannot be considered for color designated effects*. You can identify the trait's color by the **border** of the card. If a trait has more than 1 color, it is **both** or **all** of those colors at the same time.

- **Blue:** often defensive.
- **Green:** encourages growth.
- **Purple:** sneakier playstyles.
- **Red:** more risk and reward.
- **Colorless:** catch-all utilities.
