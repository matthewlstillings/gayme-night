## World's End

[[page]]

When the 3rd catastrophe is flipped, **the world ends!**

No more turns are taken. Do not rotate the 1st player.

> **Important:** Before you add up your scores, stop! There are 2 things you need to do first.

1. Check your **trait pile** for any traits with World's End effects. Look for the **World's End icon** in the lower right-hand corner of your traits. In turn order, play those effects now.
2. Then, all players follow the **gold text** on the 3rd catastrophe. *Ignore the white text.*

Now you can proceed to scoring.

## Scoring

1. Add or subtract points from the 3rd catastrophe's **gold effect**.
2. Count the **face value** of all the traits in your trait pile.
3. Count the **bonus points** on traits in your trait pile. (Look for the **Drop of Life icon** on the bottom right of your traits.)

*Doomlings also has a free online score tool that makes scoring easier, at ScoreDoomlings.com.*
