## Objective

[[page]]

Score the most points **before the world ends!**

Score points by playing **traits** over rounds called **ages**.

But watch out! Some ages are **catastrophes**.

After 3 catastrophes, **the world ends**, the game is over, and the player with the most points **wins!**

## Contents

- **113** Traits
- **40** Ages
- **6** Gene Pools
- **1** Birth of Life

## Set-Up

1. Give a **Gene Pool** card to each player. Place it in front of them with the number **5 set to the top**.
2. Shuffle all traits and deal **5 to each player**. Place the remaining traits in a pile in the center of the table to create the **trait deck** (this is where you will draw cards from).
