## How to play

[[page]]

Teams take turns, beginning with the team indicated by the triangles on the key card. Keep taking turns until one team wins.

## On your turn

### The spymaster gives one clue

If you are the spymaster, you start by trying to find a clue for 1 or more words in your team's color. More is better.

Your clue must be **1 word and 1 number**:

- The **word** relates to the words you want to give a clue for.
- The **number** tells how many words your clue relates to.

*For example, if ROOT and BARK are red words, the red spymaster might try to give a clue for both words by saying "tree: 2".*

### The operatives may make multiple guesses

The operatives should take a moment to discuss the clue. (Or, if you are the only operative, you may think out loud.) Then, one of the operatives must **make a guess by touching one of the cards**.

The spymaster covers the word with a tile matching the color shown on the key.

**If you guess a word in your own color, you may guess again.** (You get another guess, not another clue.)

*In our example, the operatives guess ROOT, so they may guess again. They then guess APPLE instead of BARK. The key card says APPLE is also red. The spymaster must act as though it was the second word intended by the clue.*
