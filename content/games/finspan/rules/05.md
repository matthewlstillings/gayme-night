## Fish Card Anatomy

Using the Great White Shark as the example:

- **Cost (upper left):** When playing this fish, you must remove 2 young from anywhere in your ocean. Also, this fish must consume (cover up) a shorter fish in your ocean. Other fish may require you to remove eggs or schools from your ocean and/or discard cards from your hand.
- **Zones (left sidebar):** This fish may be played in any zone in your ocean: Sunlight, Twilight, or Midnight.
- **Points:** This fish is worth 10 points.
- **Length (bottom left):** This is the fish's length. Large fish (like this one) have a large tail icon. Medium fish and small fish have their own, smaller tail icons.
- **Tags (next to the name):** This fish is an Über Predator since it has two predator tags. Über Predators have an ability that affects all players when played.
- **Ability (right side):** Gain this fish's benefits when you play it. Some fish abilities are activated when you dive at that fish's site (brown abilities), and others are used at the end of the game (yellow abilities). See the player aid for details about each ability type. These benefits are optional for each affected player.
- **All-players icon:** This fish's ability affects all players.

Some fish abilities reference estuaries (top row) or deepwater (bottom row).
