## Overview and Goal

*A Wingspan game. 1–5 players, 45–60 minutes, ages 10+, competitive.*

You are a marine researcher seeking to find and observe an array of aquatic life in the colorful Sunlight Zone, ghostly Twilight Zone, and pitch-black Midnight Zone of the world's seas and oceans.

In Finspan, the fish you discover over 4 weeks will generate a series of benefits as you dive deeper into the ocean. Each dive site specializes in a key aspect of expanding your research:

- Grow your collection of fish.
- Discover freshly laid eggs.
- Hatch eggs into young, and nurture the young into schools.

The winner is the player with the most points gained from fish, eggs, young, schools, and achievements.
