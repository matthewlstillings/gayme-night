## Ability Iconography

[[page]]

- **Play a fish:** Play a fish from your hand on an empty or occupied slot on your ocean mat after paying its cost.
- **Fish card:** *As a cost:* discard a fish card from your hand. *As a benefit:* draw a fish card from the deck.
- **Egg:** *As a cost:* discard an egg from a fish. *As a benefit:* put an egg on a fish (in your ocean) that doesn't already have one.
- **Hatch:** Hatch one of your eggs. (Flip over the egg token so the young side is face up.)
- **Young:** *As a cost:* discard a young from your ocean. *As a benefit:* put a young on any fish or empty slot in your ocean.
- **School:** A school forms when 3 young are on the same slot (replace the young with a school token). *As a cost:* discard a school token from your ocean.
- **Consume:** *As a cost:* the card being played must be placed on top of a shorter fish (card or forage). *As a benefit:* this fish consumes a shorter fish from your hand (tuck it underneath).
- **Fish skeleton:** Gain any card from your discard pile to your hand. If you have none remaining, draw the top card of the deck instead.
- **Egg with arrow:** Put an egg on each of your fish that doesn't already have one in the location shown or with the characteristic shown (at least one icon).
- **Move:** Move 1 young or 1 school any number of slots in a straight line in your ocean. A school may not enter or pass through a slot with another school.
- **All players:** Each player may gain the benefit shown (even if the active player chose not to gain it).
- **Color band:** You may only play this fish below the dive site that matches the color band of its ability (blue, purple, or green).
