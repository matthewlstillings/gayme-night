How to win

Score the most points after 4 weeks from fish, eggs, young, schools and weekly achievements.

FishPoints printed on visible fish, plus "game end" abilities, plus 1 per consumed fish.
Family1 point per egg and per young. Each school is worth 6.

You also score an achievement at the end of weeks 1, 2 and 3.

TiebreakMost fish cards in hand wins. Still tied? Share the victory.
The loopRulebook p. 4, 8

How a week works

You have 6 divers. Each turn, place 1 to play a fish or to dive. The week ends when everyone has used all 6.

That's 24 turns each across the game. Early on you play cheap fish and lay eggs, mid-game you play bigger fish and hatch eggs, and late on you play strong "when played" fish and build schools.

Week end Weeks 1–3, everyone at once

  1. 1Score the week's achievementPoints for each instance of the item. Write it on the scorepad.
  2. 2Side B bonusWhoever scored the most achievement points this week gains 3 more.
  3. 3Take your divers backClear them off your ocean.
  4. 4Pass the first-player tokenIt moves clockwise.

After week 4, go straight to final scoring.

Play a fish

Put a diver in the gray box at the top left, pay the fish's cost, and place it in a legal slot.

  1. 1Pay the costUpper left of the card. Discard cards from hand to your own discard pile, pay eggs or young from your ocean, or consume a shorter fish.
  2. 2Check the zoneThe left sidebar shows Sunlight, Twilight and/or Midnight.
  3. 3Check the dive siteA blue, purple or green band means it must go in the matching column. No band means any column.
  4. 4Use its "when played" abilityOptional. You may take all or part of it.
Covering tokensA fish placed on eggs, young or a school takes those tokens with it.
Bigger eats smallerRulebook p. 5

Consuming fish

A longer fish can go on top of a shorter one, by choice or because its cost demands it.

  • Length is at the bottom left of the card. The new fish must be longer.
  • Forage fish (printed on your mat) can be consumed too.
  • Tokens move up to the new fish. Cards already underneath stay there.
  • Consumed fish no longer count as fish in your ocean, but each is worth 1 point at the end.

Dive

Pick a column and move a diver from top to bottom, collecting bonuses and brown fish abilities.

  1. 1Place a diver at the topChoose 1 of the 3 dive sites.
  2. 2Take each zone's bonusYou get the bonus above a zone only if you have a fish (card or forage) in that zone of this column.
  3. 3Activate brown fishEach "if activated" fish you pass gives its benefit. Always move through all 3 zones.
  4. 4Bottom bonusFirst diver to reach the bottom of this column this week takes the empty box and the bonus. Later divers go in the gray box with no bonus.
1 egg per fishEach fish can hold at most 1 egg.
Growing upRulebook p. 6, 9

Young and schools

Hatch an egg by flipping it to its young side. 3 young on one slot become a school worth 6.

  • Forming: as soon as 3 young share a slot, swap them for 1 school token.
  • 1 school per slot. Extra young on a school's slot just sit there.
  • Moving young through a slot with 2 young (and no school) forms a school there and ends the move.
  • Schools never split back into young, and can't pass through another school.
Card colorsRulebook p. 5, 9

Fish abilities

Every benefit is optional, and you can take just part of it.

  • When played: once, right after you play the fish.
  • If activated (brown): when a diver moves onto the fish.
  • Game end (yellow): at final scoring.
  • All players: everyone may take it, even if you skip yours.

Handy to know

  • Fish skeleton: take any card from your discard pile into your hand. If it's empty, draw from the deck.
  • Your hand is face up and so is your discard pile.
After week 4Rulebook p. 8

Final scoring

First resolve any "game end" abilities that don't give points. Then, in order:

  1. Yellow "game end" points on your fish (not consumed ones).
  2. Points printed on visible fish.
  3. 1 point per consumed fish, cards and forage.
  4. 1 point per egg and per young.
  5. 6 points per school.

Add your week 1–3 achievement points.
