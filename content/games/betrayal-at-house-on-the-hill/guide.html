How to win

Explore and grow stronger until the haunt starts. Then your side, traitor or heroes, wins by finishing its haunt goal first.

Before the hauntEveryone is on the same side. Nobody can die and nobody can attack.
After the hauntOne traitor against the heroes. At least one hero must survive for the heroes to win.
Goals stay secretEach side reads only its own haunt book, and only the haunt being played.

Setup

  1. 1Set aside both haunt booksYou only need them once the haunt starts.
  2. 2Pick an explorerClip all 4 traits to their green starting values.
  3. 3Shuffle the 3 card stacksOmen, item and event, face down.
  4. 4Place the 3 starting tilesBasement Landing, Entrance Hall tile and Upper Landing, spread apart. Shuffle the other rooms face down.
  5. 5Figures in the Entrance HallThe next birthday goes first, then clockwise.

Drawing cardsRulebook p. 10

Room symbols

The first explorer to discover a room with a symbol stops there and draws.

  • Spiral, event: read it aloud, do it, then discard it unless it lasts.
  • Bull's head, item: keep it face up. Use it right away and once each turn.
  • Raven, omen: keep it. Before the haunt, you make a haunt roll at the end of your turn.
  • Still act: drawing ends your movement, but you can still use items after.
Your explorerRulebook p. 5, 12–13

Traits and damage

  • Physical: Might and Speed. Split physical damage between them as you like.
  • Mental: Knowledge and Sanity. Split mental damage between them.
  • Trait roll: roll dice equal to the trait's current value. Each die shows 0, 1 or 2.
  • Before the haunt: traits can't drop below their lowest number.
  • After the haunt: any trait reaching the skull means that explorer dies.
Things turnRulebook p. 15–16

Starting the haunt

  1. 1Haunt rollDrew an omen? Roll 6 dice at the end of your turn. Less than the number of omens drawn so far starts the haunt.
  2. 2Check the chartIn the Traitor's Tome, match that omen with your room to get the haunt number and the traitor.
  3. 3Split upThe traitor leaves with the Traitor's Tome. Heroes read the same haunt in Secrets of Survival together.
  4. 4Right NowEveryone returns and does that haunt's "Right Now" steps.
Keep it secretHeroes don't tell the traitor their goals unless the haunt requires it.
Once it startsRulebook p. 16–17

Turns after the haunt

Heroes go first, starting left of the traitor. Then the traitor, then the traitor's monsters.

  • No more haunt rolls, even for new omens.
  • Moving past opponents: leaving a room costs 1 extra space per opponent there. You can always move at least 1.
  • Say what, not why: both sides announce their actions but not their reasons.
  • Haunt rules win if they conflict with the rulebook.
FightingRulebook p. 13–14

Combat

Both sides roll dice equal to Might. The higher roll deals the difference as damage. Ties do nothing.

  • Other traits: Speed attacks deal physical damage. Sanity or Knowledge attacks deal mental damage.
  • Steal: win by 2 or more physical damage and you may take a tradable item instead.
  • Distance attacks (like the Revolver) need line of sight. You take no damage if you lose.
  • Weapons only help when attacking, one per attack.
Traitor's alliesRulebook p. 18–19

Monsters and the traitor

  • Monster movement: roll dice equal to Speed each turn.
  • Usually stunned, not killed: a stunned monster flips to "S" and misses its next turn.
  • Monsters can't explore new rooms or carry items, unless the haunt says so.
  • Traitor perks: ignore harmful room text, choose whether event cards and the Bite affect you, and steer the Mystic Elevator.
Special roomsRulebook p. 7–9

Tricky rooms

  • Barrier rooms (Chasm, Catacombs, Vault, Tower): one crossing roll per turn. Failing ends your move.
  • Coal Chute: slides you to the Basement Landing. Nobody ends a turn on it.
  • Collapsed Room: only the discoverer must roll. Later explorers may drop to the basement on purpose.
  • Mystic Elevator: roll 2 dice for the floor. It works once per turn.
  • Front door is always locked.
  • No sealing floors: a tile can't close off the last open doorway on its floor.
