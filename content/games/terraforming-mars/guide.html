The loopRulebook p. 8

How a generation works

Each round is a generation with 4 phases. The first generation skips phases 1 and 2.

Phases In this order

  1. 1Player orderFirst player marker moves 1 clockwise. Generation marker moves up 1.
  2. 2ResearchDraw 4 cards. Buy any of them for 3 M€ each and discard the rest face down. No hand limit.
  3. 3ActionTake 1 or 2 actions per turn, clockwise, until everyone has passed.
  4. 4ProductionEveryone at once. See below.

Production Everyone at once

  1. 1Energy becomes heatMove all energy cubes into your heat box.
  2. 2IncomeGain M€ equal to your TR plus your M€ production, then 1 cube per production step of each other resource.
  3. 3Reset blue cardsTake your markers off used action cards.

Playing a card

  1. 1Check the requirementNext to the cost. Only needs to be true when you play the card.
  2. 2Pay and do the effectsIn any order you like. Steel pays 2 M€ each for building tags, titanium 3 M€ each for space tags.
  3. 3Place itRed events go face down in a pile. Green cards stack showing their top row. Blue cards stay fully visible.
  • Production changes must happen. If you can't lower a production the card asks for, you can't play it.
  • Red border = any player. Red-bordered resource removal is optional and can be partial.
  • Still playable: raising a maxed parameter, adding resources you can't hold, or removing resources from no one.
  • Tags on events only count while you play them. Green and blue card tags count all game.
Unsure?Read the text in parenthesis on the card. A (*) means the card breaks a normal rule.
The mapRulebook p. 5, 15

Placing tiles

Take the placement bonus printed on the space, plus 2 M€ for each ocean next to it.

  • Ocean: only on blue spaces. +1 TR. Nobody owns it.
  • Greenery: next to a tile you own if you can. +1 oxygen and +1 TR. Worth 1 VP.
  • City: never next to another city. Worth 1 VP per adjacent greenery, anyone's.
  • Special: follow the card. 3 spaces are reserved for named cities.
Maxed oxygenA greenery still goes down and scores, but gives no TR once oxygen is at 14%.

Milestones and awards

Milestones: 8 M€, 5 VPs, first come first served

  • Terraformer: TR of 35 or more.
  • Mayor: 3 or more city tiles.
  • Gardener: 3 or more greenery tiles.
  • Builder: 8 or more building tags in play.
  • Planner: 16 or more cards in hand.

Awards: 1st gets 5 VPs, 2nd gets 2

  • Landlord: most tiles in play.
  • Banker: highest M€ production.
  • Scientist: most science tags in play.
  • Thermalist: most heat cubes.
  • Miner: most steel and titanium cubes.
Anyone can win an awardIt doesn't matter who funded it. Ties share the place, and a tie for 1st means no 2nd place. In 2-player games, 2nd place scores nothing.
All 3 parameters maxedRulebook p. 12

Game end and scoring

Finish the generation, including production. Then everyone gets one last chance to turn plants into greenery, in player order.

  1. Start from your TR.
  2. Awards: 5 VPs for 1st, 2 for 2nd.
  3. Milestones: 5 VPs each.
  4. Board: 1 VP per greenery, and 1 VP per greenery next to each of your cities.
  5. Cards: VPs from resources on cards, then all other card VPs, events included.

Setup notes and variants

  • Standard game: leave out Corporate Era cards (red and white icon). Everyone starts with 1 production of each resource and TR 20.
  • New players: take a Beginner Corporation: 42 M€ and 10 free project cards.
  • Everyone else: pick 1 of 2 corporations and keep any of 10 project cards for 3 M€ each.
  • Corporate Era: add the red and white icon cards. No starting production. Longer and more complex.
  • Draft: pass and pick research cards. Not used in generation 1.
  • Solo: Corporate Era, TR 14, no milestones or awards, finish terraforming by the end of generation 14.
Timing clash?When several effects trigger at once, the active player picks the order, even for other players' effects.
