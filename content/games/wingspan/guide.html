How to win

Score the most points after 4 rounds by building chains of birds across your 3 habitats.

BirdsEach bird on your mat scores the points printed on its card.
Everything on them1 point for each egg, cached food token and tucked card on your birds.

You also score your bonus cards and the 4 end-of-round goals.

TiebreakMost unused food tokens wins. Still tied? Share the victory.
Before you playRulebook p. 2–3

Your starting hand

Everyone gets 5 birds, 5 food (one of each type) and 2 bonus cards, then trims them down.

  1. 1Keep up to 5 birdsFor each bird you keep, discard 1 food. Keep the food your birds need (shown at their upper left).
  2. 2Keep 1 bonus cardYou can look at both bonus cards while choosing birds, and vice versa.
  3. 3Pick a goal board sideBlue (1 point per item) is gentler for new players. Green (1st, 2nd, 3rd place) is the default.
First game?Use the Swift-Start guides and the 10 gray-cornered Swift-Start birds. They walk everyone through the first few turns.
The loopRulebook p. 4–5, 11

How a round works

Take turns clockwise, 1 action per turn, until everyone has placed all their action cubes.

Turns 1 cube = 1 turn

You get fewer turns each round, because 1 cube goes onto the goal board at every round end.

  • Round 1: 8 turns each
  • Round 2: 7 turns each
  • Round 3: 6 turns each
  • Round 4: 5 turns each

Round end In this order

  1. 1Take your cubes backRemove all action cubes from your mat.
  2. 2Score the round's goalEveryone puts 1 cube on the goal board, even if they score 0.
  3. 3Refresh the bird trayDiscard the 3 face-up birds and deal 3 new ones.
  4. 4Pass the first player tokenIt moves clockwise.

Play a bird, or use one of your 3 habitat rows. The more birds in a row, the stronger it gets.

How a row action works

  1. 1Place your cubePut it on the leftmost open slot in that row and gain that slot's benefit.
  2. 2Slide it right to leftActivate each brown "when activated" power you pass. Every power is optional.
  3. 3Leave it at the far leftYour turn is over.
Paying for birdsRulebook p. 6

Food and egg costs

Food is printed on the bird. Eggs depend on which column you play it into.

  • Egg cost by column: column 1 is free, columns 2–3 cost 1 egg, columns 4–5 cost 2 eggs. Take eggs from any of your birds.
  • Food: pay from the food next to your mat, never from food cached on birds.
  • Wild food (multicolored circle) can be any type. A slash means "or". A crossed-out circle means no food cost.
  • Max 5 birds per habitat.
2-for-1When playing a bird, any 2 food can stand in for any 1 food. This trade only works when playing birds.
Food diceRulebook p. 7

The birdfeeder

Take 1 food token per die you remove. Dice stay out until the feeder is refilled.

  • Invertebrate/seed face: gain 1 of either, not both.
  • Empty feeder: roll all 5 dice back in.
  • All dice show the same face (even just 1 die)? Before gaining food for any reason, you may reroll all 5.
Card colorsRulebook p. 10

Bird powers

The power's color tells you when it works. All powers are optional.

  • Brown, "when activated": fires right to left when you use that bird's habitat.
  • Pink, "once between turns": fires on other players' turns, once between each of your turns.
  • White, "when played": fires once, when you play the bird.

Words to know

  • Cache: put a food token on a bird. You can't spend it, but it's worth 1 point.
  • Tuck: slide a card under a bird to make a flock. Each tucked card is worth 1 point.
  • Star nest: wild. It counts as any nest type.
End of each roundRulebook p. 11

Scoring round goals

Count the item the goal asks for, then mark your score with a cube.

  • Blue side: 1 point per item, max 5.
  • Green side: rank players 1st, 2nd and 3rd. You need at least 1 of the item to score.
  • Green ties: add the points for the tied places, split them evenly and round down. Skip the next place.
After round 4Rulebook p. 5

Final scoring

  1. Points printed on each bird on your mat.
  2. Your bonus cards.
  3. Your end-of-round goals.
  4. 1 point per egg, cached food and tucked card.
Play betterRulebook p. 3–4, 10

Tips

  • Pick starting birds that get you cards or food. Brown powers are especially helpful early.
  • Repeating an action is fine. Your first turns are simple while you attract a few birds.
  • Remind each other about pink powers. Tell the table what yours do and what triggers them.
