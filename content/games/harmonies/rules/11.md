## Solo mode

*You can play solo to discover the game, practice, or simply try to beat your highest score! To play alone, the following changes are necessary:*

### Setup

Place the Central board with the Solo side faceup, which only has 3 spaces. Place only 3 Animal cards faceup near the Central board.

### Game turn

At the end of each turn:

- Return the remaining 6 tokens to the box (they are discarded and are not returned to the Pouch), then refill each of the 3 spaces with 3 tokens.
- If you did not take an Animal card on this turn, you may discard 1 Animal card from the center of the table and replace it with the top card from the draw pile.

**All other rules remain unchanged.**

### Suns

When playing solo mode, your goal is to earn as many suns as possible, representing your level of success during the game. The number of suns obtained depends primarily on your score, but is also influenced by the side of the Personal board used, as well as the Nature's Spirit you chose at the start of the game. Try to beat your high score in each configuration.

**Score:**

- **40 points:** 1 sun.
- **70 points:** 2 suns.
- **90 points:** 3 suns.
- **110 points:** 4 suns.
- **130 points:** 5 suns.
- **140 points:** 6 suns.
- **150 points:** 7 suns.
- **160 points:** 8 suns.

**Board side:** side A adds 1 sun. Side B adds none.

**Nature's Spirit:** playing without one adds 2 suns. A Spirit that scores groups of connected Landscapes adds 1. A Spirit that scores the number of Landscapes adds none.

*Example: Pauline scored 130 points (5 suns) while using side A of the Personal board (+1 sun) with the Lion's Spirit (+1 sun). Thus, she earned 7 suns. Not bad!*
