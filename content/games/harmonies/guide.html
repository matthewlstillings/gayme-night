Start hereRulebook p. 2, 7

How to win

Build the best landscape on your board and settle as many animals in it as you can. Most points wins.

LandscapesTrees, mountains, fields, buildings and water each score on their own.
AnimalsEach animal card scores the number in its topmost empty cube space.
TiebreakMost animal cubes placed wins. Still tied? Share the victory.

Setup

  1. 1Central board2–4 player side up. All tokens in the pouch.
  2. 2Fill the 5 spaces3 random tokens from the pouch on each.
  3. 3Animal cardsShuffle and deal 5 face up. Cubes within reach.
  4. 4Personal boardsEveryone uses the same side. Side A for your first games. Take the matching reminder card.
  5. 5First playerWhoever last saw a magnificent landscape. Point the central board's arrow at them.
First gamesLeave the Nature's Spirit cards and cubes in the box.
When it's youRulebook p. 4–6, 12

Your turn

Take and place 3 tokens (must). Take 1 animal card and place animal cubes (may). Any order.

ActionHow oftenWhat it does
Take and place tokensMandatoryOnceTake all 3 tokens from one central space and place them on your board.
Take 1 animal cardOptionalOncePick 1 of the 5 face-up cards and fill its spaces with cubes. Max 4 unfinished cards.
Place animal cubesOptionalUnlimitedMove a card's bottom cube onto a matching habitat on your board.
  • Mix it up: you can do optional actions between placing your tokens.
  • End of turn: refill the empty central space with 3 tokens from the pouch, and refill the animal row to 5.
Placing tokensRulebook p. 4

What can stack

  • Mountains: 1, 2 or 3 gray.
  • Trees: green alone, or green on 1 or 2 brown.
  • Buildings: red on 1 gray, red or brown.
  • Water and fields: 1 blue or 1 yellow. Never stacked.
NeverPlace under existing tokens, or on (or under) an animal cube.
Placing cubesRulebook p. 5–7

Placing animals

Build the card's pattern on your board, then put a cube on the marked token.

  • Any rotation of the pattern counts.
  • Heights must match for trees and mountains. Buildings can sit on any color.
  • 1 cube per token, but a token can be part of several habitats.
  • Finished card: move it beside your board. It no longer counts toward your 4.
Cubes stay putA cube is never removed, even if a later token breaks its habitat.
Last roundRulebook p. 7

When the game ends

The end triggers in one of two ways. Finish the round so everyone has the same number of turns.

  • Pouch is empty when you need to refill the central board.
  • 2 or fewer empty spaces on a player's board at the end of their turn.

Once you know itRulebook p. 10–11

Nature's Spirit and solo

  • Nature's Spirit: deal 2 each, keep 1 on your first turn and put a spirit cube on it. It counts toward your 4-card limit until placed, and scores extra points at the end based on your landscapes.
  • Solo: use the 3-space solo side of the central board and 3 animal cards. After each turn, discard the 6 leftover tokens to the box and refill. If you didn't take an animal card, you may swap one out.
  • Solo suns: 40 points is 1 sun, up to 8 suns at 160. Side A adds 1. No spirit adds 2.
