Start hereRulebook p. 3, 6

How to win

Fill rows on your wall and score the most points. The game ends after the round in which someone completes a full horizontal row.

During playEvery tile you place on your wall scores right away.
At the endBonuses for full rows, full columns and all 5 of a color.
TiebreakMost complete horizontal rows wins. Still tied? Share the victory.
Before you playRulebook p. 2

Setup

  1. 1Player boardsColored-wall side up for everyone. Scoring marker on 0.
  2. 2Factory displaysIn a circle: 5 for 2 players, 7 for 3, 9 for 4.
  3. 3BagAll 100 tiles go in (20 of each color).
  4. 4First playerWhoever most recently visited Portugal takes the starting marker.
  5. 5Fill the factoriesThe first player puts exactly 4 random tiles from the bag on each display.
Big pictureRulebook p. 3

Each round has 3 phases

  1. AFactory offerTake turns picking tiles until every display and the center are empty.
  2. BWall-tilingEveryone at once: move tiles from complete pattern lines to the wall and score.
  3. CNext roundThe first player refills every display with 4 tiles, unless the game has ended.
Who startsThe starting marker goes to the center at the start of each round. The first player to take from the center gets it.
Factory offerRulebook p. 3–4

Your turn

Take every tile of one color from one place, then put them all in one pattern line.

Take fromWhat happens
A Factory displayTake all tiles of one color. Push the rest of that display into the center.
The centerTake all tiles of one color. First one to the center this round also takes the starting marker onto their floor line.
  • Right to left: fill your chosen pattern line from its right end.
  • One color per line: a line that already has tiles only takes more of that color.
  • Not if it's on the wall: you can't use a line whose wall row already has that color.
  • Leftovers fall: tiles that don't fit go to your floor line, left to right.
Any timeYou can put tiles straight on your floor line instead, if you'd rather take the penalty.
End of the roundRulebook p. 4

Wall-tiling

  1. 1Top to bottomFor each complete pattern line, move its rightmost tile to the matching color on that wall row. Score it now.
  2. 2Clear those linesThe other tiles from those complete lines go to the box lid.
  3. 3Keep the restIncomplete pattern lines stay as they are for next round.
  4. 4Floor lineLose points for each tile there, then clear it to the lid. Keep the starting marker in front of you.

Running lowRulebook p. 5

When the bag runs out

  • Bag empty: pour every tile from the box lid back in and keep filling displays.
  • Lid empty too: start the round anyway with some displays short.
Once you know itRulebook p. 6

Gray-side variant

  • Any space: a tile can go on any space of its wall row.
  • No repeats: no color twice in any row or any column.
  • Stuck tile: if a complete line's tile has no legal space, its whole line goes to the floor.
