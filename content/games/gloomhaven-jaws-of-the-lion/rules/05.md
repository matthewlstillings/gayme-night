## Scenario Book Features

The Scenario Book holds several key pieces of information, marked A to K on the sample spread:

- **A. Scenario number, title and city map board location**, for reference.
- **B. Goal of the scenario.** Achieving it completes the scenario successfully. In Scenario 1, every monster on the map must be killed.
- **C. Scenario key**, listing the monsters from the game box and the tokens and tiles from the token tray needed to build the map. Scenario 1 needs only the Vermling Raiders.
- **D. Map layout.** Here it just shows that the scenario fits on the Scenario Book's two-page spread. Later scenarios may also call for a separate page from the Supplemental Scenario Book.
- **E. Introduction text.** Read it now.
- **F. Special rules text.** Read it after the introduction. It adds information that the normal rules do not cover.
- **G. Conclusion text.** Only read it once the scenario has been completed successfully.
- **H. Rewards** for completing the scenario. Read these after the conclusion.
- **I. Tips and rules reminders.** Consult these during the scenario as needed.
- **J. Obstacle designations** on the scenario map, shown by a green border. Obstacle hexes cannot be entered.
- **K. Wall designations.** Every scenario's hexes are enclosed by a thick, dark border line. These lines are the scenario's walls and cannot be crossed.
