## Playing the Game

[[page]]

A scenario is played as a series of **rounds**. Each round, every player picks the ability cards they want to play. Those cards set the "initiative order": the order in which characters and monsters take their **turns**. When every **figure** on the map (that is, every **character** and **monster**) has had a turn, the round is over and a new one starts.

Each round always goes in this order:

1. Card Selection
2. Ordering of Initiative
3. Character and Monster Turns
4. End of Round

## Card Selection

All players, at the same time and in secret, choose two ability cards from their six-card hand and lay them face down in front of them. While choosing, players may talk strategy and describe their plans in general terms, but may not show their cards or reveal any specific numbers or card names.

One of the two cards is chosen as the **initiative card**. The initiative value printed in the middle of that card (1) sets the character's place in the round's order: **lower numbers act first**. Put the initiative card face down under the other card, so that when the pair is turned over, the initiative card is on top and its initiative value shows.

When the character's turn arrives, they perform the top action (2) of one card and the bottom action (3) of the other card, **in any order they like**.

[[fig p06-ability-card-anatomy | Ability card parts: initiative, top and bottom actions, text boxes]]

The plain-language descriptions in the blue boxes on each card (4) explain what its actions do; the details are covered in the following pages. If you are not sure what to pick, try these cards:

- **Hatchet:** Center Mass, Double Throw
- **Demolitionist:** One-Two Punch, Knock Out the Support
- **Voidwarden:** Suggestion, Wicked Scratch
- **Red Guard:** Blinding Sickle, Shocking Advance

Which of the two cards becomes your initiative card is your choice. The round plays out from lowest to highest initiative, and Scenario 1's special rules have the monsters act at initiative 50. So you are really deciding whether to act before the monsters (a lower number) or after them (a higher number).

The icons at the middle left of the cards (5) can be ignored for now; Scenario 2 explains them.
