## Play Area Setup

[[page]]

Use the character materials and other components to set up Scenario 1.

Each player puts in front of them their character mat (1), player reference card (2), six level "A" ability cards (3), attack modifier deck (the deck of smaller cards) (4) and hit point dial (5). Turn the red side of the dial to the red number printed below the white "1" along the bottom of the character mat. Ignore the dial's blue side for now.

[[fig p04-health-track | Level 1 health track (Red Guard)]]

*In this example, the Red Guard's level 1 health is 10.*

Put each character's initiative order token (6) near the middle of the table.

Open the Scenario Book to Scenario 1 (7) and lay it in the middle of the table. It shows the scenario map, made up of **hexes (hexagons)**, where the scenario is played.

Each player places their character figure on any one of the starting hex icons (8) in the Scenario Book. No two characters may share a hex.

From the "Vermling Raider" monster zip bag, take out the cardboard standees, the large square stat card and the initiative order token. Put the initiative token with the others (6). The smaller cards in the bag are not used in this scenario. Slide the stat card into one of the six-section envelopes from the box so that the side with a "1" in its upper left corner shows (9), and put it beside the Scenario Book.

Put the monster standees into the white and gold plastic stands from the game box, then place monsters on the map in the Scenario Book (10) using these rules:

1. The monster icon on a hex tells you which set and rank of monster goes on that hex. Read the top row with two characters, the middle row with three characters and the bottom row with four characters.
2. In that row, a black bar means no monster is placed for that player count; white means a normal monster (standee in a white stand); gold means an elite monster (standee in a gold stand). Every standee has a number on it; pick standee numbers at random when placing them.
