## Damage and Killing Monsters

[[page]]

Once an attack's final value is worked out (first apply any bonuses, then the attack modifier card), the target suffers that much damage. When a monster suffers damage, put that many damage tokens on its stat envelope, in the section matching the number on the monster's standee.

[[fig p09-damage-tokens | Damage and condition tokens on a stat envelope]]

If the damage tokens there now equal or exceed the monster's maximum hit points (see page 10), the monster is killed and taken off the map. Return all tokens from that monster's envelope section to the tray.

### Line-of-Sight

To target any figure with an ability, the targeting figure needs a clear line-of-sight to it. Line-of-sight exists if a line can be drawn from any part of the targeting figure's hex to any part of the target's hex without touching a wall line. **Only walls block line-of-sight.** Obstacles and other figures do not.

> NOTE: The rules from here up to "Monster Turns" on the next page cover more situational character effects. You can start taking character turns now and look these up when they come up. When initiative 50 arrives and the monsters act (if they haven't already), go on to "Monster Turns" on page 10.

### Target

Some attacks are followed by "Target X" (the Target icon, concentric circles): the attacker may target X different enemies within the attack's range. One ability can never attack the same enemy more than once. Draw a separate attack modifier card for every separate attack.

### Suffer Damage

Some abilities make figures suffer damage without making an attack. That damage is not modified and ignores line-of-sight.

### Conditions

A condition can be applied as an ability on its own or as an effect of an attack. Conditions are still applied even if the attack deals no damage. Scenario 1 uses four conditions: three negative and one positive.

When a character gains a condition, put its token in the lower left section of their character mat. When a monster gains one, put the token on the stat envelope in the section matching the monster's standee number. All four of these conditions remain until the end of the target's next full turn, which may be later in the same round, or in the next round if the target has already acted this round.

[[fig p09-condition-icons | Condition icons]]

#### Negative conditions

- **Immobilize:** the figure cannot perform any Move abilities.
- **Stun:** the figure cannot perform *any* abilities at all, including moving or attacking.
- **Muddle:** all of the figure's attacks gain Disadvantage.

#### Positive condition

- **Strengthen:** all of the figure's attacks gain Advantage.
