## Ordering of Initiative

[[page]]

When everyone has chosen, turn each character's cards face up to reveal their initiative. Work out the initiative order by comparing each character's initiative card value with the monsters' initiative (always 50 in Scenario 1). Line up the initiative order tokens from lowest to highest.

**Ties:** if a character and a monster tie, the character acts first. If two characters tie, compare the initiative values on their second cards.

Then play out the round, beginning with the figure that has the lowest initiative.

[[fig p07-initiative-example | Revealed cards and the resulting initiative order]]

*Example: once the cards are revealed, the Red Guard (initiative 14) acts first, then the Demolitionist (20), then the monsters (50), then the Hatchet (64).*

## Character Turns

With the order set, each figure takes one turn in that order. On their turn, a character performs one top action and one bottom action; never two tops or two bottoms. At this point it no longer matters which card was the initiative card: either card may be used first, for either its top or its bottom action.

An **action** is a whole half of a card. Its contents must be done in the order printed; afterwards, the card goes into the character's discard pile, to the left of their character mat.

An action can be made up of several **abilities**, separated by a dotted ability line. Any ability, or a whole action, may be skipped.

The ability types follow, so the first character in the order can take their turn. If the Vermling Raiders act first, jump to "Monster Turns" on page 10 and come back here when a character's turn comes up.

### Move

A "Move X" ability (the Move icon, a winged boot) lets a figure move **up to** X hexes; it does not have to use all of its movement. Figures may move through their allies (characters through characters, monsters through monsters), but may never end their movement in an occupied hex (one with another figure in it). Figures cannot move through enemies, obstacles or walls.
