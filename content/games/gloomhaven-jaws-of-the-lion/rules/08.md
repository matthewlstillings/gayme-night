## Attack

[[page]]

An "Attack X" ability (the Attack icon, a sword with a burst) lets a figure deal a base X damage to one targeted enemy within its range. A figure cannot attack its allies unless an effect says otherwise. Attacks are either **ranged** or **melee**.

A **ranged** attack has "Range X" (the Range icon, a bow) printed under it: it can target one enemy within X hexes of the attacker. Range may not be counted through a wall. An Attack without the word "Range" under it is a **melee** attack and can only target adjacent enemies, unless an effect says otherwise.

[[fig p08-range-example | Range 2 example]]

*Example: the Demolitionist (a) is within Range 2 of the monster (1).*

Besides what they can target, the big difference between the two is this: a ranged attack against an enemy adjacent to the attacker gains Disadvantage (see Advantage and Disadvantage, below).

### Attack Modifier Cards

Whenever a figure attacks, it turns over one card from its attack modifier deck for every separate target, to modify that attack.

1. A number card: add or subtract its value from the attack value.
2. A "2x" card: double the attack value.
3. A null card (a circle with a slash): the attack deals no damage.
4. The 2x and null cards also carry a shuffle symbol. If either one is drawn, shuffle the entire deck at the end of the round.

[[fig p08-modifier-deck | The 20-card starting attack modifier deck]]

Every character has an attack modifier deck of their own; all the monsters share one deck. In Scenario 1, every deck is identical, with 20 cards: six +0, five -1, five +1, one -2, one +2, one 2x and one null.

### Advantage and Disadvantage

Some effects, such as the conditions described on the next page, can give an attack Advantage or Disadvantage.

- **Advantage:** the attacker draws two attack modifier cards and uses the better one.
- **Disadvantage:** the attacker draws two cards and uses the worse one.

An attack can never have more than one instance of Advantage or of Disadvantage. If an attack has both, they cancel out.

A ranged attack made against an adjacent enemy gains Disadvantage.
