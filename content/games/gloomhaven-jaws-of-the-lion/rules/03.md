## Scenario 1 Rules

[[page]]

The Scenario Book's **Rewards** box for Scenario 1 lists a new location: Roadside Ambush (1), at map square B1.

[[fig p03-map-sticker | Placing the first sticker on the city map]]

That reward means: peel the first sticker on the sticker sheet ("Roadside Ambush") and stick it on square **B1** of the city map board, lining up its artwork with the board.

*The stickers are permanent. If you don't want to alter your copy, skip them and keep track of which scenarios are open some other way.*

The city map board shows which scenarios you can currently play. Right now only Scenario 1 is open; more will open up as the campaign goes on. Put the map board aside for now.

### Choosing characters

Before starting Scenario 1, each player picks a character. There are four **characters**, so up to four people can play.

> NOTE: **Playing by yourself?** A solo player must run two characters at once, which is a more demanding way to play. With two to four players, each player should control just one character.

To pick, find the large and small boxes marked with the four character icons and take out what is inside:

[[fig p03-character-roles | The four characters and their roles]]

- **Demolitionist:** Melee damage and obstacle destruction
- **Red Guard:** Protection and monster manipulation
- **Hatchet:** Ranged damage and looting
- **Voidwarden:** Healing and support

The small boxes hold the character figures. The large boxes hold character mats (artwork on the front, a character description on the back) and the character's card decks.

When everyone has chosen, each player takes for their character:

- their **figure** and **mat**
- the rectangular **initiative order token**, from the character's zip bag
- a **hit point dial**, from the game box
- the **deck of smaller cards with no warning** on it
- the **first seven cards from the larger deck with no warning** on it. One of these is a **player reference card**; the other six are **ability cards**, each with an "A" in the crown beneath its title.

Leave the two decks that carry warnings, and the rest of the larger deck, in the character's box for now. They will not be used yet.
