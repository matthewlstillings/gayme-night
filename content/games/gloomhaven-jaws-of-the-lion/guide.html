How to win

Everyone wins or loses together. Each scenario has its own goal, printed in the Scenario Book.

WinMeet the scenario's goal, like killing every monster or the boss. Then finish the round.
LoseEvery character is exhausted. A character is exhausted at 0 hit points or when they can't play 2 cards.
Lost one?You keep the money, experience and treasure you earned. Everything else resets, and you can try again.
Before each scenarioRulebook p. 3–5, 17, 25

Setup

  1. 1Your characterMat, reference card, ability cards, your attack modifier deck and a hit point dial set to your level's max.
  2. 2Initiative tokensOne per character and per monster type, near the middle of the table.
  3. 3The mapOpen the Scenario Book to the scenario. Each character stands on a starting hex, one per hex.
  4. 4MonstersOn each monster icon, read the row for your player count: white is normal, gold is elite, black is none. Standee numbers are random.
  5. 5Stat cardsSlide each monster type's card into an envelope with the scenario level showing.
  6. 6Monster decksShuffle the shared 20-card "M" modifier deck. From Scenario 2, shuffle each monster type's ability deck too.
  7. 7From Scenario 4Element board, all tokens Inert. Deal 2 battle goals to each character; keep 1 in secret.
Playing soloRun two characters. With 2 to 4 players, each player runs one.
Big pictureRulebook p. 6–7, 12, 17, 22, 26

Each round has 4 steps

  1. 1Card selectionAt the same time and in secret, each player picks 2 cards from hand, or declares a long rest. Talk strategy, but no card names or numbers.
  2. 2InitiativeReveal. Your lead card's number is your initiative. Flip one ability card for each monster type on the map. Lowest number goes first.
  3. 3TurnsEvery character and monster type takes a turn in initiative order.
  4. 4End of roundShuffle decks, wane elements and short rest. See below.

Round end In this order

  1. 1ShuffleAny modifier deck that drew a 2x or null card, and any monster ability deck that drew a shuffle card.
  2. 2Elements waneEach element moves one column left: Strong to Waning, Waning to Inert.
  3. 3Short restAnyone with 2 or more discards may short rest.
TiesA character beats a monster. Two characters compare their second cards.
CharactersRulebook p. 6–7, 15, 20

Your turn

Do the top half of one card and the bottom half of the other, in either order. Never two tops or two bottoms.

  • In order: do an action's abilities in the order printed. You can skip any ability or a whole action.
  • Basic actions: any card can be played as Attack 2 on top or Move 2 on the bottom instead. It's discarded.
  • Lost icon: if you use an action with it, the card goes to your lost pile, not your discard. Lost cards don't come back this scenario.
  • Experience: mark the XP shown only when you actually do part of that ability. No XP for basic actions or for kills.
  • Loot: at the end of your turn you pick up any money token or treasure in your hex.
AbilityWhat it does
Move XUp to X hexes. Pass through allies, not enemies, obstacles or walls. Never end in an occupied hex.
JumpMove over figures, obstacles, terrain and traps. Not through walls. A trap where you land still springs.
Heal XYou or one ally in range. Never above max hit points.
Loot XEvery money token and treasure in your hex and within range X.
Shield XReduce each attack against you by X, after modifiers. Stacks. Attacks only.
Dealing damageRulebook p. 8–9, 15–16

Attacks

Add your bonuses to the attack, then draw one modifier card per target.

  • Melee: adjacent enemies only.
  • Ranged: any enemy within range. Count range around walls, not through them. Only walls block line of sight.
  • Ranged at an adjacent enemy: you have Disadvantage.
  • Target X: X different enemies, one modifier card each.
  • Area attacks: a grey hex makes it melee. A ranged area only needs one red hex in range.
  • Kills: a monster dies when its damage reaches its max hit points.
ModifierEffect
+1, −1 and so onChange the damage by that much.
2xDouble the damage. Shuffle at round end.
Null0 damage. Effects like conditions still apply. Shuffle at round end.
AdvantageDraw 2, use the better. If it's unclear which is better, pick either.
DisadvantageDraw 2, use the worse. If unclear, use the first card drawn.
Starting deckSix +0, five +1, five −1, one +2, one −2, one 2x and one null. Advantage and Disadvantage don't stack and cancel each other.
Save yourselfBefore any one source of damage hits you, you can lose 1 card from your hand or 2 from your discard to ignore all of it.

Conditions

They apply even when the attack does 0 damage. Unless noted, they last until the end of the target's next full turn.

ConditionEffect
ImmobilizeCan't move.
DisarmCan't attack.
StunCan't use any abilities.
MuddleDisadvantage on all attacks.
StrengthenAdvantage on all attacks.
PoisonUntil healedAttacks against it get +1. A heal only removes the poison.
WoundUntil healed1 damage at the start of each of its turns. A heal removes it.
CurseUntil drawnA null card shuffled into its modifier deck. 10 at most.
BlessUntil drawnA 2x card shuffled into its modifier deck. 10 at most.

Monster turns

Every monster of a type acts on that type's initiative: elites first, then normals, each in standee number order.

  1. 1Find its focusThe character it can attack with the least movement. Tie: the one earliest in initiative. Ignore line of sight and assume unlimited movement.
  2. 2MoveOnly if that gets it closer to attacking its focus. If paths tie, the players choose.
  3. 3AttackIts stat card plus the ability card. Draw from the monster modifier deck.
  • No "Move" on the card: it doesn't move. No valid hex to attack from: it does nothing.
  • Ranged monsters: move only as far as they need, and step away from an adjacent focus to avoid Disadvantage.
  • Traps: monsters avoid them unless a trap is the only way. Then they take the path with the fewest traps.
  • New room: its monsters can act this round if their initiative comes after the character who opened the door.
  • Elements: monsters always consume if they can, and every monster of that type gets the bonus.
  • Bosses: use the boss ability deck and the Scenario Book's special rules. Their stats often scale with the number of characters, and they are immune to some conditions.

Elements

An element icon on its own means you infuse it. An element icon with a red X means you may consume it for a bonus.

  • Infuse: do any part of that action. The element goes to Strong at the end of your turn.
  • Consume: only from Strong or Waning. Move it to Inert and get the bonus after the colon.
  • Not on the same turn: you can't infuse an element and use it in one turn.
  • Wild icon: on a monster card, the players choose which element.
Getting cards backRulebook p. 12, 22

Resting

Rest to get your discards back. Every rest costs you one card for the rest of the scenario.

RestHow it works
Short restEnd of roundNeeds 2 or more discards. Shuffle them, lose 1 at random, take the rest back. Once per short rest, you may suffer 1 damage to lose a different random card instead.
Long restYour whole turn, initiative 99Declare it at card selection. Choose 1 discard to lose, take the rest back, Heal 2 yourself and refresh your spent items.
Running outIf you can't play 2 cards at the start of a round, you're exhausted. Monsters still act while you long rest.

Between scenarios

  1. 1RefreshEveryone returns to full health with all cards and items back.
  2. 2GoldEach money token you looted is worth the scenario level's gold value (2 at level 1). Gold is yours alone.
  3. 3ExperienceWrite down what you marked, plus the scenario's bonus XP. XP never resets.
  4. 4Battle goalsMet yours? Gain 1 check mark. Every 3 check marks is a perk. Lost the scenario? Nothing.
  5. 5City eventAfter a win, draw one, pick an option together, then read the outcome.
  6. 6Shop and level upBuy, sell or trade items. Level up if you've hit the next threshold.

  • Level up: add 1 card of your new level or lower, gain 1 perk, and raise your max hit points.
  • Items: buy at cost, sell for half rounded up. 1 head, 1 body, 1 feet, 2 hand and small items equal to half your level, rounded up.
  • Using items: free on your turn. Consumed items work once per scenario; spent items once per long rest.
  • Scenario level: average character level divided by 2, rounded up. Easy −1, Hard +1, Very Hard +2.
