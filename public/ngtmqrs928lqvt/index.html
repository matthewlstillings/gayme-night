Leonida Field Guide

GTA 6 beginner guide

0% tracked

Play GTA 6 without losing anything for good.

The game has no mission replay, and most of what you can lose sits in Chapter 1. Set where you are below, and this guide shows only what still matters.

Where are you in the story?
Saved on this device. Later chapters stay blurred so nothing gets spoiled.
TL;DR: save by hand before every chapter finale, bank your cash, buy (don't loot) the guns you love, and don't chase big money until Chapter 3.

01First 10 minutes

Fix these settings before you play.

Camera and controls

  • Motion Blur Strength → 0 (Settings › Display).
  • Look and Aim Sensitivity → 9 or 10 (Settings › Controller).
  • Movement Speed → Run (Settings › Gameplay).
  • Lock the minimap on screen, and switch units (feet/meters, °F/°C) under Unit Settings.

The prologue

Play it in one sitting. Save Game is grayed out for the whole prologue.

Make your first manual save the moment it unlocks.

Fun extra: escape the store robbery on a Seashark jet ski instead of the road for unique dialogue.

02Saving: your only undo

There's no mission replay. Your saves are the replay.

Autosave only runs after a mission ends. You can't save mid-mission.

Save manually: Pause › Game › Save Game. There are 15 slots.

Use a fresh slot every chapter, and one more right before each finale.

Save before random encounters, too. Dying in a side event costs the cash you carry.

Cheats unlock after the story. They switch off trophies and saving for that session, so use a throwaway save.

03Money: three pockets

Where your money sits decides whether you keep it.

PocketSafe?What to know
Cash on youNoLost when you're busted or downed. Cap is about $20,000; you can't pick up more. Shops take cash only up to about $1,000.
Jason's floor stash → BuckMeYesDrop cash under Jason's bedroom floor. After the Buck Me mission, deposit at any ATM. Big buys come out of BuckMe.
Family SavingsLockedThe only money that survives Chapter 1. You can't withdraw it. Put at least $5,000 in before Tourist Trap.

Early money that's worth it

  • Shirley's rum errand: about $2,600. Parrot cage vent (~$1,300) plus the back-room safe (~$1,300).
  • Kenton's treasure dives after Island Dreams: the first wreck pays about $1,400.
  • Check Jason's mailbox. Mission pay and Brian's lockup payouts land there.
  • Brian's lockup code: 7700. Drop loot in his strongbox; he pays later.

When the real money starts

  • Chapters 1–2: enough for guns and clothes. Don't grind.
  • Chapter 3: jewelry stores ($10,000–25,000), Randy's Wish List cars ($30,000+), small banks after Credit Check.
  • End of Chapter 4: large bank vaults, reported up to $205,000 each.
  • Fences pay differently. Compare offers in the map's robbery index, or ask your partner to sell (a bit less, no trip).

04Guns and gear

Only guns you buy at Ammu-Nation are yours. They come back after death and sit in every store's weapon locker.

Looted guns vanish when you're busted or downed. Keep favorites in your car's trunk.

Carry limit: two sidearms and two long guns.

Buy the long gun in Gear Up at Chuck's Tackle & Shot in Key Lento. Skipping it can stall the Feng Shui call.

Holster with L1/LB. A drawn gun makes bystanders call the cops.

Suppressors delay the police, but don't stop every report.

05Police and heat

Stars are back (up to six), and cops now track what they know about you.

Don't get reported

  • Rob stores clean: cut the back-room alarm and destroy the tapes.
  • Cloud cameras can't be destroyed. Police can ID you from them later.
  • Wear a mask until you're out of the area.
  • Shower after a shootout. Blood makes cops suspicious; shop restrooms work too.

Lose them fast

  • Leave while police are still en route to the scene.
  • Change your look: keep a spare outfit in the trunk (up to five), respray or swap the car, holster.
  • Drive off a bridge into water. Your personal car survives sinking.
  • Disable, don't kill: shoot tires, or aim at yellow zones in combat Focus.

06Criminal Profile

A hidden rating per character, like RDR2's honor. One tier is permanent.

TierYou get here byWhat changes

HardenedKilling armed enemies who fight backNormal cops. You can surrender.
ViolentHeadshots, finishing downed enemies, killing copsStill recoverable, but the climb back is slower.
PsychoKilling pets, crowds of civilians or policeNo surrender. Cops may shoot on sight.
IrredeemableStaying Psycho too longPermanent for that character. Strains Jason and Lucia.

Mission-required kills and true self-defense don't count. Watch for the warning mark on the Psycho icon: Irredeemable is close.

07Your body: Health, Stamina, Focus

Train a little every day. Strength and cardio have daily caps, so short daily sessions beat one long one. Skipping too long loses levels.

Protein shake or bar from Jason's kitchen, then sleep, speeds recovery.

Each character trains separately.

Focus is the slow stat. Fishing gives about 10 per catch. Basketball, pool, mini golf and the shooting range help too.

Press L3+R3 to scan: it highlights cash, loot, safe-code notes and tracks, and points you at strange sounds.

Coffee and tea refill Focus faster.

08Cars and getting around

Your personal car is your horse. It holds long guns, outfits and loot, and its trunk is safe when you go down.

After Just a Scratch, call Joey (Coastal Auto Care). He delivers it repaired, free, even from the bottom of the sea.

Pick the color carefully: that respray is the only free one.

Scan before you steal with the WAiNK app: tracker, sell value, and register cost.

Register only cars you love through Moses (Chapter 2). Unregistered cars are gone if lost. Buy garages after Daily Driver.

The Slim Jim is slower but silent and keeps the car's value.

No fast-travel menu. Hail a ride in the RideMe app: tip $5 (up to twice) to go faster, or pay $100 to skip the trip.

Autopilot: hold ↓ on the D-pad for the cinematic camera and the car drives to your waypoint.

Scooter Bros app delivers, repairs or pulls your car from impound. One player paid $121 for a delivery and $698 for an impound pickup.

Watch fuel and engine condition. Gas stations fix small damage; auto shops fix the rest.

09Jason and Lucia

Hold → on the D-pad for partner commands: create a distraction, take the wheel, or sell your stash.

Switch characters after Breakfast Run: hold → and press R1/RB.

Inventories are separate. Ammo and healing items don't share. A bought gun works for both, but each customizes it.

Relationship goes up with greetings, drinks, holding hands and time together. Ignored calls and long splits lower it.

11Explore like you mean it

  • Stop for weird sights and sounds. A car with an open door and a trail of clothes led one player to a UFO mission.
  • Scan every car and animal you pass. It counts toward trophies and 100% later.
  • Sell collectible cards at the pawn shop, or keep sets for the trophy.
  • Hold off on robberies until you can play as both. One does crowd control while the other empties the safe.

Before you hit a point of no return

Run the matching list right before each finale. Every one starts with a save.

100% completion tracker

Nothing on the 100% list is missable, and most items need only part of the total. The in-game list appears after the story under Progress › Stats › 100% Completion; story progress already counts.

Reaching 100% unlocks A Long Road (Gold), which the Platinum needs. Type your counts in as you go.

Trophies you can lose
